Reading the diagram. Follow the numbers 1 → 17. Each phase adds a new tier of people allowed to invite and a higher bar for newcomers. Phase 4 (right-most column) is the steady-state path most developers will take and the one flagged for the VTI-stack experience study, because it has the most steps and the “gather-then-present” friction. Acceptance is decided automatically by the Policy Engine — there is no manual gatekeeper.
